# Howr

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who want an audible sense of passing time while working, with controls available from the menu bar.

## Product Purpose

A small macOS menu-bar app that chimes every 60, 30, or 15 minutes. Users choose sounds, volume, active hours and weekdays, and can pause or skip a chime.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, nostalgic, compact. The existing interface draws on the LCD digital watch described in the README.

## Anti-references

Preserve the current digital-watch identity rather than returning to the earlier generic analog-clock concepts in design/variation-*.png.

## Design Principles

- Keep the menu-bar origin visible in product demonstrations.
- Preserve the black case, blue trim, pixel labels, and seven-segment clock.
- Use the established sage daylight and cyan backlight palettes.
- Describe only features implemented in the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing app provides keyboard navigation, accessible control labels, and spoken time descriptions. Marketing images are decorative; retain real text and controls when implementing a landing page.
