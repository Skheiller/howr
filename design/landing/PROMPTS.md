## 01-sage-menubar-hero.png

Opaque PNG composition.

### Production prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Goal: final high-quality PNG landing-page artwork for Howr, a tiny macOS menu-bar hourly chime app. Reference images show the actual shipped app; faithfully preserve its distinctive tall rectangular watch-inspired software popover, proportions, thin black resin-look bezel, fine blue inset case line, red LIGHT marker, blue MODE marker, pale blue HOWR · HOURLY CHIME label, pixel-font menu rows and seven-segment numerals. This is a SOFTWARE POPOVER, not a wristwatch, phone or tablet. No wrist strap, no watch lugs, no crown, no thick hardware sides. Subtle depth only from the shallow UI bezel and art-directed soft shadow. Keep the whole popover fully visible unless specifically asked for a macro. The LCD is one continuous screen with clock and menu on it. Preserve reference controls, no invented features.
Exact visible UI text: case "HOWR · HOURLY CHIME", "LIGHT", "MODE"; screen "TU 27", large "2:42" and small "PM", "NEXT 3:00"; rows "[ PAUSE 1H ]", "[ TIL TOMORROW ]", "[ SKIP NEXT ]", "[ TEST ]", "VOL" with ten-segment bar and "60", "SETTINGS", "QUIT". Reproduce these once per popover, using dot-matrix small labels and real seven-segment clock shapes. Black LCD ink, restrained faint inactive segments. No marketing text, watermark, extra logos or product names. No unrelated objects. Premium tactile software campaign artwork with disciplined composition, credible surface light and sharp legible UI.
Canvas and composition: landscape 16:10. A desktop-context hero, spacious tonal sage-green background (#aeb99d), faint broad soft-focus topographic wallpaper forms in matching olive tones, restrained and almost flat. One straight-on app popover on the right half (center near x=72%), taking about 70% of canvas height. A narrow real macOS-style menu-bar strip spans the canvas near y=12%; make it look like part of the desktop screenshot, not a detached physical bar. The popover descends from a small bell icon in that strip, with a tiny connecting pointer. Minimal system status icons and short time only on the right; avoid lots of menu text. LEFT 45% is clean calm negative space for later HTML headline. Match the existing daylight LCD (#b0ba9e) closely to the background, black case remains contrasting. Soft afternoon light, grounded natural shadow, restrained product-ad composition. No laptop or display frame, no headline baked into the artwork.
